About Us

Protecting Under the Hard Hat is a collaborative initiative between the construction industry and NJIT that connects construction workers and their families with resources for mental health, substance use, and overall well-being. Through a brief survey and resource platform, we aim to make it easier to find support and access help when it is needed. As the initiative grows, we continue to expand our resources, partnerships, and outreach to better serve the construction community.

Our Mission

Protecting Under the Hard Hat is dedicated to improving access to mental health, substance use, and wellness resources for construction workers and their families. Through education, outreach, and resource navigation tools, we aim to make it easier to find support, reduce barriers to seeking help, and promote a healthier construction workforce.

Our Goals

Our goals are to increase awareness of mental health and substance use challenges within the construction industry, reduce stigma surrounding help-seeking, and connect individuals with trusted resources and support services. We strive to foster a culture in which mental well-being is valued alongside physical safety and where workers know that help is available when they need it.

Roni Barak Ventura

Assistant Professor

School of Applied Engineering and Technology, Newark College of Engineering, New Jersey Institute of Technology

Dr. Roni Barak Ventura is an Assistant Professor in the School of Applied Engineering and Technology at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT). Her research focuses on designing human-centered technologies that promote health, well-being, and social good. By combining engineering, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction, she develops technologies that are not only effective but also relatable, trustworthy, and responsive to people's needs.

Her work spans digital health, rehabilitation engineering, and assistive technologies, including the development of telerehabilitation systems and other innovative tools to improve access to care. Through Protecting Under the Hard Hat, she works with construction industry partners to apply these engineering approaches to support construction workers and their families, creating evidence-based technologies that connect people with mental health, substance use, and wellness resources.

Dr. Barak Ventura's work is informed by both scientific research and her personal experience with depression and recovery, strengthening her commitment to reducing stigma and making it easier for people to seek help. Her broader research also uses data science and causal inference to better understand complex public health challenges and evaluate interventions and policies that improve health and well-being.

Chris Lalevee

Member Assistance Coordinator

International Union of Operating Engineers Local 825

Chris Lalevee is a Business Agent and Member Assistance Program (MAP) Coordinator for the International Union of Operating Engineers (IUOE) Local 825, representing thousands of heavy equipment operators throughout New Jersey and New York's Hudson Valley. A second-generation Operating Engineer and former crane operator, Chris transitioned from the field into union leadership, where he champions the health, safety, and well-being of construction workers.

Drawing on his own lived experience, Chris developed Local 825's confidential Member Assistance Program to connect members and their families with resources for mental health, substance use, and other personal challenges. He is also a founding member of the Alliance for Naloxone Safety in the Workplace, working with labor organizations, contractors, and industry leaders to promote overdose prevention, workplace naloxone programs, and supportive workplace policies.

A nationally recognized speaker and advocate, Chris collaborates with industry partners to integrate mental health, addiction recovery, overdose prevention, and suicide prevention into everyday construction safety, helping create workplaces where workers feel supported and empowered to seek help.
